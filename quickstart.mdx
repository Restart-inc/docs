---

This guide walks you through the fastest path to a running system on Restart Console. By the end, you'll have a live system configured and visible in your monitoring dashboard.

<Note>
  Every action in this guide is also available programmatically through the Restart API. Visit the [API Reference](/api/introduction) to manage systems, settings, and integrations directly from your own code or automation pipelines.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to the Console">
    Open [app.restart.inc](https://app.restart.inc) in your browser and enter your credentials.

    - If this is your first time, select **Sign up**, complete the registration form, and verify your email address.
    - If your organization uses SSO, select **Continue with SSO** and authenticate through your identity provider.

    After signing in, the Console loads your **Systems** dashboard automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first system">
    From the **Systems** dashboard, select **New System** in the top-right corner.

    1. Enter a unique **System Name**.
    2. Choose a **Region** closest to your infrastructure.
    3. Select a **System Type** that matches your use case.
    4. Select **Create System**.

    <Tip>
      Pick a descriptive name you'll recognize quickly in the dashboard — you can always rename the system later from its **Settings** page.
    </Tip>

    The new system appears in your dashboard with a **Provisioning** status. It transitions to **Active** within a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the system settings">
    Select your new system from the dashboard to open its detail view, then navigate to the **Settings** tab.

    Review and update the following options to match your requirements:

    - **Environment variables** — add any key-value pairs your system needs at runtime.
    - **Resource limits** — set CPU and memory boundaries to control costs and performance.
    - **Access controls** — specify which operators and builders can view or modify this system.

    Select **Save Changes** when you're done. Settings take effect immediately without requiring a restart.

    <Warning>
      Changing resource limits on a running system may cause a brief interruption. Schedule limit changes during a low-traffic window when possible.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor your system status">
    Navigate to the **Monitoring** tab on your system's detail page to see live metrics.

    - **Status** — shows whether the system is Active, Degraded, or Offline.
    - **Uptime** — displays the rolling percentage for the last 30 days.
    - **Event log** — lists recent state changes, errors, and integration events in chronological order.

    <Info>
      You can set up webhook notifications to push status-change events directly to your alerting tool. See [Webhooks](/integrations/webhooks) for setup instructions.
    </Info>

    Your system is now live and being monitored. From here you can connect integrations, invite teammates, or explore the API.
  </Step>
</Steps>

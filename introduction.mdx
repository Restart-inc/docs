## Get started in three steps

Follow the steps below to go from a fresh account to a live, connected system.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to the Console">
    Navigate to [app.restart.inc](https://app.restart.inc) and sign in with your credentials. If you don't have an account yet, select **Sign up** and complete the registration flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first system">
    From the **Systems** dashboard, select **New System**, give it a name, and choose your initial configuration. Your system is provisioned immediately and appears in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect integrations">
    Open your system's **Integrations** tab and link the external services your system depends on. Each integration can be configured, paused, or removed at any time without downtime.
  </Step>
</Steps>
